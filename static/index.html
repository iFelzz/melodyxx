Free YouTube to MP3 Converter & Music Downloader

Melodyxx is an ultra-fast, zero-disk web application designed to convert and download YouTube videos to MP3 audio files with studio-grade 320kbps fidelity. Unlike traditional online converters cluttered with intrusive popups and download redirects, Melodyxx operates on a modern Cobalt-style streaming pipeline that pipes audio on the fly directly to your device with 0 MB server storage.

How to Download Music from YouTube in 3 Simple Steps:

  1. Search or Paste: Enter your favorite song title, artist name, or paste a YouTube video or music URL into the search command bar.
  2. Choose Quality: Select your preferred audio bitrate—320kbps for maximum studio clarity, 192kbps for everyday listening, or 128kbps for compact data-saving files.
  3. Stream or Download: Click "Play" to preview the audio instantly in the browser mini player, or click "MP3" to initiate an immediate high-speed download with official Apple Music 800x800 album art and clean ID3v2 tags.

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Apple Music Artwork Integration: Resolves official high-resolution album cover art and metadata automatically for clean music library indexing.
  • Full Artist Discography Browser: Explore top popular singles, albums, and EPs for any music artist with one-click sequential batch download queues.
  • Zero-Disk Streaming Architecture: Real-time in-memory FFmpeg audio transcode piping with zero temporary buffer files stored on server disks.
  • 100% Free & Ad-Free: No deceptive advertisements, third-party trackers, or account registration required.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading music with Melodyxx.

How do I convert and download YouTube to MP3?

Type a song title or artist into the search box above, or paste any YouTube video link. Pick your desired audio quality (128k, 192k, or 320k), then click Download. Melodyxx instantly pipes the converted MP3 file directly to your browser.

Are album covers and ID3 tags embedded in the audio?

Yes. Melodyxx automatically queries Apple Music to match track titles and embed official 800x800 square cover artwork, song title, artist, album name, and release year directly into the ID3v2 metadata frames.

Can I download multiple songs or entire albums at once?

Yes. Click the "Artists" search tab to explore discographies. Check the boxes next to the songs you want—or click "Select All"—then hit "Download Selected" to sequentially download the entire batch.

What is the highest audio quality bitrate available?

Melodyxx offers 320kbps (best high-definition fidelity), 192kbps (optimal balance of size and quality), and 128kbps (lightweight data saving). You can easily switch quality using the global toggle at the top of the search results.
